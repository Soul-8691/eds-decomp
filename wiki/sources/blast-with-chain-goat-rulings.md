# Blast with Chain Goat Format Rulings

Source: `Blast_with_Chain.txt` (repository root, supplied by the user on 2026-10-29). It's a reply from the r/Goat_Format
thread "Blast with Chain" (reddit.com/r/Goat_Format/comments/zgjcvl), answering: "If my opponent destroys my face-down
Blast with Chain and I chain it and equip one of my monsters with it, can I pop one or does it miss the timing? What
if I equip Gearfried with Blast with Chain in the same position?"

## What it says

- **Mandatory.** The destruction effect is mandatory, so it can't miss timing. It activates from the Graveyard in the
  next available chain. Chaining a Set Blast to the Mystical Space Typhoon / Heavy Storm / Breaker / Mobius / Dust
  Tornado that destroys it equips it first, so the destruction pops it.
- **Damage Step.** Blast can be activated in the Damage Step, because it changes battle stats.
- **Lost with its monster.** If the equipped monster is destroyed by battle, Blast is destroyed by game mechanics and
  does not activate.
- **Gearfried.** In Goat Format, Gearfried the Iron Knight's effect is continuous: a Blast equipped to it is destroyed
  at once, by a card effect, while equipped, so it pops. Uses:
  - against an attack on Gearfried, to destroy the attacker;
  - chained to a Ring of Destruction on Gearfried, so Blast resolves first and pops.
  Modern rules differ: there Gearfried's effect is a trigger.
- **No equip, no pop.**
  - With chain link 1 Blast on Gearfried and chain link 2 Ring of Destruction on Gearfried, Gearfried is gone when
    Blast resolves; Blast "fizzles" and goes to the Graveyard by game mechanics.
  - Solemn Judgment, or Royal Decree negating it (already face up, or chained to it), means it never equipped.
- **Royal Decree first.** With chain link 1 Royal Decree and chain link 2 Blast on Gearfried, Blast equips and dies
  first, and its pop activates in the Graveyard, where Decree (which negates Traps on the field) does not reach.
- Effect templating and timing ("If A: B" mandatory; "When A: you can B" can miss timing) and SEGOC ordering.
- **Replays.** Goat Format replays go back to the start of the Battle Step (another monster may attack first); modern
  replays go back to the same monster's attack declaration.
